Sixteen paintings, from the inside

Imagining Artist Methods

How did a painting get made? Each guide takes one famous work and walks through what the artist's process would have been, from training and first decisions to the last stroke, using technical studies, documents and the drawings, cartoons and other versions that survive.

Where the evidence runs out, the guides say so. Each chapter opens with a note on what it rests on, and every AI-generated image is labelled.

How these guides work

Evidence first

Each chapter opens with a note on what it rests on: a technical study of the painting, documents, or the general practice of the period. Inference is labelled as inference.

Intermediate sources

The drawings, cartoons, letters and other versions that survive are shown with their collections and credits.

AI reconstructions

The stages in between were generated with Prodia from the real painting and kept in register with it. They show plausible appearances, not documented ones.

Open images

The paintings are shown from public-domain photographs, mostly from Wikimedia Commons and museum open-access collections. A few scientific images appear under Creative Commons licences, credited where they appear.
